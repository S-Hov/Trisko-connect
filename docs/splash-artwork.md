## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: original commissioned-looking abstract artwork for the startup splash of Trisko connect desktop software, comparable in art direction quality to artwork featured on professional creative software splash screens. This is artwork, NOT a photograph of an office and NOT a UI mockup.
Primary request: create an arresting, sophisticated abstract graphic artwork, like a bespoke contemporary design-studio poster. Sculptural interlocking broad ribbons and precisely folded planes form a dynamic connected structure, inspired by the folded curved ribbon of the supplied application icon. The connected structure quietly suggests access and paths converging into one workspace.
Reference image: existing blue Trisko application icon, a formal reference for folded-ribbon geometry and a restrained cornflower blue accent only. Do not reproduce the icon in the image; it will be overlaid as the exact existing asset.
Style/medium: art-directed mixed-media graphic design combining precise cut-paper collage, expressive broad flowing ribbons, hand-printed ink textures, fine visible paper grain and restrained halftone. Beautifully balanced, confidently designed, contemporary editorial poster. Deliberate graphic shapes rather than generic smooth CGI. Clearly intentional crafted design.
Composition/framing: landscape 16:10. A striking complex yet legible sculptural ribbon composition occupies the right 65%, with dramatic overlapping cropped sweeps extending off the right and lower edges. Keep the left 35% largely quiet deep graphite with subtle paper texture, for real application typography to be overlaid. Have a few thin elegantly curved contour lines following ribbon paths. Controlled asymmetry, generous negative space, strong silhouette. This is a single cohesive finished artwork.
Color palette: charcoal black and warm graphite background, chalk ivory and silver grey broad ribbons, restrained cornflower blue sections echoing the reference icon, a tiny muted terracotta accent. NO blue background.
Lighting/mood: depth from printed shadows, tonal layering and paper folds, not glossy 3D lighting. Sophisticated, expressive, architectural, premium. Real tactile imperfections in ink and edges, crisp intentional geometry.
Text: none. No lettering.
Constraints: no office, no interiors, no buildings, no people, no monitor icons, no folders, no literal networking diagram, no padlock or shield, no generated logo, no watermark. Do not imitate or include any Adobe or Corel trademark.
Avoid: generic AI art, neon, cyberpunk, sci-fi glow, holograms, rainbow gradients, chrome liquid blobs, plastic glossy 3D, stock corporate illustration, clutter, tiny floating spheres, pseudo-technical gibberish.
